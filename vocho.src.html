La premisa The premise

El coche que nunca se fue

The Beetle rolled off the line at Puebla in 1964 and became something its engineers never drew: a piece of Mexican identity. Shortened to vocho, it carried families, taxi fleets, and six decades of street life.

Nobody has rebuilt it to the standard the car deserves — real craft, real engineering, and a story that could only come from here.

So we are building it. One at a time, in the Bajío, with the trades that have been here longer than the car has.

El taller The workshop

Cada lámina fuera

Nada al tanteo.

Four stages, one car at a time, in a single queue. The exterior reads exactly as it should. The platform underneath does not.

I

Despiece Strip

The shell comes off the pan and goes to bare metal. Everything that was ever bolted on is catalogued, tagged, and either restored or replaced. Nothing is painted over.

II

Lámina y acabado Metal & finish

Corrected, refinished, and put back true. Chrome re-plated, glass replaced, whitewalls and roof rack correct to the period. The car has to be right before it is interesting.

III

Primero Drivetrain

The pan comes back with a different heart. Our own conversion system — engineered, not adapted — badged and finished to the same standard as the coachwork around it.

IV

Guarnición Trim

Palm, leather, textile, brass. The last stretch is the slowest, and it is the part an owner touches every day. Done by hand, by people whose families have done it for generations.

El interior The craft

El oficio es el argumento

Hand-woven palm on the dash. Saddle leather where vinyl was. Regional weaving set into the seats, from the same trades the Bajío has run for generations. This is where “built in San Miguel” stops being an address and becomes the product itself.

Primero The platform

Plataforma propia

Diseñada, no adaptada.

A purpose-built conversion system, badged and finished to the same standard as the coachwork around it. Real range, real torque, serviceable in-market.

The exterior looks backward. Everything underneath is pointed the other way.

Named for its place as the first proprietary system in the house — the spine every future platform will share.

2

3

The primero cover — cast, then chased with the house rosette.
